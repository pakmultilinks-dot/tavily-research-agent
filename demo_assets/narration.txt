[SEGMENT 1]
You walk into a meeting, and the other side has moved. A new launch, a new hire, a funding round, and you never saw it coming. That is the problem Tavily Research Agent solves.

[SEGMENT 2]
Give it a company or a person, and a meeting date. It searches the last thirty days only, across news, launches, hires, and funding. No more scrolling through weeks of headlines the night before. It hands you a one page briefing, with a summary, key developments, discussion points, and open questions, so you walk in prepared.

[SEGMENT 3]
Here is the real thing running. This is the Python app, and this run rebuilds the live briefings from sixty real Tavily search results gathered on November fifth, twenty twenty six. It filters the thirty day window, drops duplicates, and drops any result whose source link cannot be verified.

[SEGMENT 4]
The results are real. OpenAI, fourteen developments kept, including the Pierre acquisition on November fourth and the GPT-6 launch. Google, fifteen kept, including the Gemini workplace agent and the universal shopping cart. Microsoft, eight kept, including the buyouts of sixty thousand workers and a twenty five billion dollar debt raise. Every development carries its date and its source link.

[SEGMENT 5]
We are honest about the limits. The snippets are short extracts from the Tavily Playground, and the direct Tavily API route still returns an error in this environment, so the live data here comes from the Playground, not a direct API call. Nothing in these briefings is invented.

[SEGMENT 6]
The code, the tests, and the pitch deck are public on GitHub, at github dot com slash pakmultilinks-dot slash tavily-research-agent. Twelve tests pass. Try it before your next meeting, and walk in prepared.
